Part 2: Mathematics & Entropy

Duration: 1.5 Hours

Entropy and Randomness

A cipher is only as strong as the randomness of its key.

  • PRNG (Pseudo-Random Number Generator): Deterministic algorithms. Predictable. Fine for simulations, catastrophic for cryptography.
  • CSPRNG (Cryptographically Secure PRNG): Possesses the Next-Bit Test property. Unpredictable even if internal state is partially known.
